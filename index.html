04 FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Everything you need to know before reserving your whale shark experience in Bohol.

What should I bring for the whale shark tour?

Bring swimwear, reef-safe sunscreen, a towel, and anything you personally need for an early-morning trip. We recommend keeping valuables to a minimum.

What time does the tour depart?

Whale shark experiences operate in the early morning. Your exact pickup or meeting time depends on the tour type and operating conditions for the day.

Is the tour suitable for children?

Children can join when the selected tour and current sea conditions are suitable. Please include children's ages when making an inquiry so we can advise you on the best option.

How far in advance should I reserve?

Advance reservation is recommended because departures are early and daily operating capacity can be limited. Same-day requests may not always be possible.

Where can I check the latest operating conditions?

Check the Today's Whale Shark section above for the latest field updates. Conditions can change, so the latest report should be checked before departure.

What happens if weather or sea conditions are not suitable?

Safety comes first. If conditions are not suitable for operation, the trip may be adjusted, postponed, or cancelled according to the day's operating decision.
