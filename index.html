03 THE EXPERIENCE

Meet the ocean's
gentle giant.

The whale shark is the largest fish in the ocean — a calm, filter-feeding giant that moves through warm tropical waters. Despite its enormous size, it feeds mainly on tiny plankton and other small organisms.

Seeing one in the water is a rare reminder of how extraordinary the marine world can be. The best encounter is a respectful one: observe, give it space, and let the animal decide where to go.

RHINCODON TYPUSThe world's largest fishPhoto: Derek Keats / Pexels

OUR OCEAN, OUR RESPONSIBILITY

Protect the giants.
Preserve the blue.

Whale sharks are wild animals, not attractions that can be directed or controlled. Their future depends on healthy oceans and responsible human behavior.

Every visitor has a role to play. Keep a respectful distance, follow the crew's instructions, and never interfere with a whale shark's natural movement. A good memory should never come at the expense of marine life.

BEFORE YOU ENTER THE WATER

Safety & encounter
guidelines.

These simple rules help keep you safe while reducing stress and disturbance to whale sharks and other marine life.

✓
RESPONSIBLE BEHAVIOR

DO'S

  • Pay close attention to your guide, boat crew, and local authorities before and during the activity.
  • Wear your life jacket and snorkeling gear correctly whenever required.
  • Enter and leave the water slowly, and only when your guide gives permission.
  • Keep at least 5 metres from the head and 6 metres from the sides and tail of the whale shark.
  • Stay calm and quiet. Let the whale shark swim freely without trying to follow it.
  • Choose reef-safe sunscreen, or avoid applying sunscreen immediately before the tour where possible.
  • Respect all marine life and comply with marine park rules throughout the visit.
×
WHAT TO AVOID

DON'TS

  • Never touch, ride, chase, corner, or obstruct a whale shark.
  • Do not feed wildlife or throw food, rubbish, or other objects into the sea.
  • Avoid flash photography, underwater lights, and bright strobes.
  • Do not shout, splash excessively, or make sudden movements that may disturb marine life.
  • Do not collect corals, shells, or any other marine organisms.
  • Never jump from the boat unless your guide specifically instructs you to do so.
  • Do not enter the water without clearance from your guide or boat crew.
!

PLEASE READ BEFORE BOOKING

Important information

  • Whale sharks are wild. Sightings are not guaranteed on every trip.
  • Tour operation may change due to weather, sea conditions, or local marine regulations.
  • For your safety and the animals' welfare, follow all instructions from your guide and crew.
  • Guests participate at their own risk and are responsible for following the safety briefing.
  • Anyone who ignores safety instructions or harms/disturbs wildlife may be asked to leave the activity without a refund, subject to applicable law and the operator's policies.

Thank you for helping protect whale sharks and the ocean they call home. Responsible encounters today help preserve these remarkable animals for tomorrow.

05 FAQ

Good to know
before you go.

Quick answers about choosing your tour, getting ready, and enjoying a safe whale shark encounter in Bohol.

Which whale shark tour should I choose?

Choose Ticket Only if you will arrange your own transportation and guide. Pick-up & Drop-off is a join-in option with round-trip transfer from the Jumbo Crab meeting point. Private Tour includes hotel pick-up and drop-off with a dedicated guide, snorkeling equipment, underwater photography and light snacks. The Whale Shark + Napaling option includes the private tour features plus snorkeling at Napaling point.

What is included in each tour?

Inclusions differ by package. Please check the tour comparison table and the individual tour page before booking. Ticket Only covers the whale shark admission ticket; transport, guide service and equipment are not included in that option.

Where do I meet the team?

For the join-in Pick-up & Drop-off tour, the meeting and drop-off point is Jumbo Crab restaurant. For Private Tour and Whale Shark + Napaling, hotel pick-up is arranged in advance. Ticket Only guests make their own way to the whale shark site.

What time does the tour start?

Whale shark activities generally take place early in the morning. Your exact meeting or hotel pick-up time will be confirmed with your booking details, as it can vary by tour and operating conditions.

Can I touch or swim close to a whale shark?

No. Never touch, chase, ride, feed, or block a whale shark. Keep at least 5 metres from its head and 6 metres from its sides and tail, and always follow the local guide and crew instructions. The encounter should be calm and respectful.

Are whale shark sightings guaranteed?

No. Whale sharks are wild animals, and sightings cannot be guaranteed. Their presence depends on natural conditions, so please understand that this is a wildlife encounter rather than a guaranteed animal show.

Is underwater photography included?

Underwater photography is included in the Private Tour and Whale Shark + Napaling packages. It is not included in Ticket Only or Pick-up & Drop-off.

What happens if the weather or sea conditions are poor?

Guest safety and marine protection come first. Tours may be delayed, adjusted, postponed or cancelled depending on weather, sea conditions and local operating decisions. Please follow updates and instructions from our team.

Can children join the tour?

Suitability depends on the child's age, comfort in the water, the selected package and the conditions on the day. Please include each child's age in your inquiry so our team can check the appropriate option.

How can I change or ask about my reservation?

Message our team on WhatsApp with your booking name, tour date and the change or question you have. Any change is subject to availability and the applicable booking terms.
