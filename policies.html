01 / TERMS

Terms and Conditions

These Terms and Conditions apply to reservations made with BS TRAVEL AND TOURS, operating under the brand Bohol Whale Shark in Bohol, Philippines.

Reservations & Payment

  • Reservations are confirmed only after full payment has been completed.
  • Prices are displayed and charged in Philippine Pesos (PHP).
  • Payments are processed through PayMongo and may be made using supported methods such as Visa, Mastercard, GCash and Maya.
  • Customers are responsible for providing accurate booking, contact and accommodation information.

Tour Operation

  • Participants must be at least 4 years old.
  • Pregnant participants may join only with appropriate medical clearance from a doctor.
  • Non-swimmers may participate; life jackets are provided.
  • Customers should bring appropriate swimwear.
  • For transfer-inclusive products, pickup and drop-off are available within Panglao Island.
  • Schedules, departure times and activities may be adjusted due to weather, sea conditions, safety requirements or operational circumstances.
By completing a reservation and payment, the customer acknowledges these terms and agrees to follow reasonable safety instructions given by Bohol Whale Shark and its operating partners.

03 / REFUNDS

Return and Refund Policy

Because our products are scheduled tourism services, cancellation refunds are based on the time remaining before the scheduled tour date.

Cancellation timingRefund
15 days or more before tour100%
8–14 days before tour80%
4–7 days before tour50%
Within 3 days of tourNon-refundable

Date Changes

Date changes are available when requested at least 7 days before the scheduled tour date and are subject to availability.

Weather / Sea Conditions

If Bohol Whale Shark cancels the tour because weather or sea conditions make the operation unavailable or unsafe, the customer will receive a full refund.

Operator Cancellation

If the tour is cancelled by Bohol Whale Shark for operational reasons, the customer will receive a full refund.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within approximately 1 week.

The refund terms above do not limit any mandatory consumer rights or remedies that may apply under Philippine law.
