GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does Ticket Only include transportation?

No. Guests arrange their own transportation to and from the whale shark site.

No. A tour guide is not included in the Ticket Only option.

Is snorkeling equipment provided?

No. Snorkeling equipment is not included. Please bring your own equipment if you need it.

What do I need to do at the whale shark site?

Present the ticket you received after purchase at the whale shark site before entering. Follow the instructions of the on-site staff.
